# 🏦 Bank Switch & Finance Incentives Log — NGN

Track Nigerian bank switching bonuses / interest-promo offers and cashback works.

## How switching works in Nigeria

- Retail banks occasionally run promo offers ("open account + fund → bonus naira"). These are less standardized than UK CASS. Verify conditions with the bank:
  - Eligibility (new business vs salary)
  - Minimum funding balance
  - Direct-deposit requirement (e.g. salary inflow NGN 50k+ for 3 months)
  - Bonus cash credited vs points
- **Keep a log of every offer you chase** — banks may claw back if conditions are missed (survival risk).
