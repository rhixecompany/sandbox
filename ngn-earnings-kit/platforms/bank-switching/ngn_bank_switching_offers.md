# NGN Bank-Switching & Account-Opening Offers — Nigeria (mid-2026)

> Researched: August 2026. Currency: NGN. Baseline: US$1 ≈ ₦1,500.
> **Reality check first:** no Nigerian bank pays a guaranteed cash sign-up bonus like the UK's Current Account Switching Service (CASS). Nigerian "switching" incentives are almost entirely (a) **lottery draws** for saving/transacting (GAIM, DiamondXtra, Save&Win), (b) **referral rewards** on fintech apps, or (c) **interest/perks** on salary accounts. Treat any viral "₦15,000 sign-up bonus" post as suspect — most are old campaigns or lead-farm SEO.
> Verified = official bank/app page, press release, or reputable media. Community = user reports/social (weaker). (unverified) = could not confirm.

## C. REALISTIC EXPECTATION — what a site supervisor (steady salary) should actually do

1. **Guaranteed money is referral money, not bank money.** Banks (Access, Fidelity, Union, FirstBank, Zenith, GTB, UBA) pay *nothing* in guaranteed cash for opening accounts; they sell lotteries ("win ₦10m!") that need you to save/transact — fun, but expected value ≈ 0 unless you're the lottery winner. Do not restructure your banking for a draw.
2. **Real cash, in order of reliability (mid-2026):**
   - Kuda Premium/Plus referrals — **₦4,000–₦12,000 per referral** (Tier-2 referee) — most concrete recurring.
   - OPay — ₦800/referral, small but no deposit needed beyond funding.
   - PalmPay — welcome up to ~₦5,550 while campaigns live + ₦250/pair referral.
   - Rise (RDC) — up to 4% on Naira Vault referrals, official.
   - PiggyVest — ₦1,000 + ₦1,000 (you must park ~₦2,100 to unlock).
   - Moniepoint — ₦4/transaction, 12-month tail.
3. **Salary-side value (bigger than any bonus):** Zenith salary account 7.95% p.a. + salary advance; UBA zero-balance opening + LEO coins; GTBank free transfers forever; banks' save-promotions require only ₦5–10k min deposit — use spare savings in Fidelity GAIM-style accounts.
4. **Protect**: never pay a fee for a bonus; never "activate codes" from unknown Telegram/WhatsApp; ignore "switching kit" claims (Nigeria has no CASS-like switch, only new-account opening); West African banking scam pages love "₦15k NextGen bonus" graphics.

**One-line summary:** The genuine NGN things are fintech referral programs (Kuda, Rise, OPay, PiggyVest, PalmPay) and bank deposit-lotteries (Fidelity GAIM, Access XtraWins, Union Save & Win) — the referral ones are cash-certain but require real invitees; the bank ones are lottery tickets on active savings behavior.
