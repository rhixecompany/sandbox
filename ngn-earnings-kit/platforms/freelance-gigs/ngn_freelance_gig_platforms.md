# NGN Earnings Kit — Freelance & Gig Platforms (Nigeria · Site Supervisor / Facilities / HSE profile)

**Profile:** Site Supervisor/Manager — Rexos Properties (May 2025 – Jun 2026): construction, property, facilities management, health & safety.
**FX assumption:** US$1 ≈ ₦1,500 (mid-2026). All figures in NGN or original currency with NGN equivalent.
**Compiled:** August 2026. **Verified** = confirmed from source; **(unverified)** = not independently confirmed; **(self-reported)** = platform's own marketing numbers.

## 0. NGN payout rails — Nigeria reality check (read first)

| Rail | Works for receiving? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Payoneer** | ✅ Yes — the workhorse | Withdraw USD balance to any NGN bank. Mar-2025 fee structure: $4 flat per transfer under $400; 1% above; USD→NGN conversion markup ≈ 2% all-in. Source: payoneer.com/about/pricing; vaultleap.com/blog/payoneer-withdrawal-conversion-fees-2026 |
| **Cleva / Grey / Raenest (USD 'virtual US account')** | ✅ Yes | Nigerian fintechs issue real US account/routing numbers in your name. Upwork "Direct to US Bank (ACH)" deposits land free (~$0.99), convert to NGN in-app at interbank-ish rate in minutes. Cleva claims zero Upwork deposit fees (verified as product claim). Source: getcleva.com/blog/how-to-receive-payments-from-upwork-in-nigeria-in-2025 |
| **Wise** | ⚠️ Partial | Wise receives USD *account details* are NOT offered to Nigeria residents (USD details unavailable in some regions — Nigeria is one). NG users get NGN balance for holding/sending, not a USD inbound rail. Use Wise to convert/exit NGN or to receive from clients who pay direct to Wise (NGN details exist at wise.com/us/account/ngn-account). |
| **PayPal (NG)** | ❌ Not for receiving | Nigerian PayPal accounts are send-only; receiving remains restricted (2026 guides confirm). PayPal "renewed interest" in NG was reported 2025-26 but not an official receive service — do not build on it. Sources: hurupay.com/blog (2026), paypal.com/ng |
| **Payoneer company rails (Fiverr Revenue Card / Local Bank Transfer)** | ✅ | Fiverr pays you via Payoneer Revenue Card (US$1–3) or LBT (US$3). Same Payoneer-to-NGN bank exit. |
| **USDC/stablecoins** | ✅ | Contra & Cleva support USDC; convert to NGN via crypto OTC/exchanges. Useful, not primary. |
| **Flutterwave / Paga** | ❌ Not personal freelance rails | Merchant tools. **No evidence** of an active "Paga gig" freelance marketplace as of mid-2026 — treat as dead/unverified lead, do not plan around it. |

**Bottom line:** set up **Payoneer** + one **USD virtual account (Cleva/Grey/Raenest)** + your NGN bank account. That combination covers every platform below.

## 1. Global freelance platforms (USD earners → NGN)

### 1.1 Upwork — ✅ most viable global platform for this profile
- **What it is:** world's largest freelance marketplace; escrow-protected hourly/fixed contracts.
- **Typical rates:** Nigerian freelancers average **~$163/job** (Nairametrics 2024, via getcleva.com). Beginner band $10–25/hr; project/PM work typically $19–45/hr globally (upwork.com/resources/upwork-hourly-rates). Middle East & Africa median ≈ $40/hr (gigradar.io). NG construction-related profiles already live at $30/hr (upwork.com/hire/construction-management).
- **Demand for your skills:** Real and growing in *remote-doc* form: construction estimating, quantity takeoff (79 open "on-screen takeoff" jobs), project management, property/facilities virtual assistance, HSE documentation. Live examples: construction estimators at $30–$85/hr (upwork.com/freelancers/*).
- **Entry barrier:** Low-ish, but Connects (bidding credits) cost money (~$0.15 each, bundles required) and starting profiles win slowly. Identity (ID+BVN) verification required. No geo-block for NG.
- **Fees:** Freelancer service fee 0–10% per contract (set at offer, ~10% typical since 2025; gigradar.io). Withdrawal: Direct to Local Bank (NGN): **$0.99 + FX spread, up to 4-5 business days** (support.upwork.com); Payoneer ~2–3% all-in; Cleva USD (ACH) 0 deposit fee.
- **Protection:** Escrow on fixed-price; hourly tracker screenshots; dispute + mediation.
- **First 90 days (realistic):** new construction profile = 0–2 contracts/mo. Month 1: **$0–$100 (₦0–150k)**; month 3: $150–$500/mo (₧225k–750k) if niche pricing undercuts US at $25–40/hr. Long/slow-burn: yes, but highest ceiling. **Verdict: top-tier, start now.**
- Sources: upwork.com/resources/upwork-hourly-rates; support.upwork.com; getcleva.com/blog (Cleva, NG avg $163); gigradar.io/blog/upwork-withdraw-money-fastest

### 1.2 Fiverr — ✅ viable second platform (documentation-type gigs)
- **What it is:** catalogue marketplace; buyers buy "gigs"; no bidding.
- **Typical rates:** Nigerian sellers commonly at $5–$50/gig → **₦7.5k–75k/gig** (headhunter.ng). Top sellers ₦250k/mo+ (same source, unverified).
- **Your service angles:** "HSE safety plan & toolbox talk packs", "construction progress report template", "property maintenance checklist", "virtual site supervision reports", remote assistant PM gigs. Demand for construction/FM categories is *thin* vs design/coding — expect 1-3 orders/month after a 4-8 week ramp with SEO'd gig titles.
- **Entry barrier:** Free; needs compelling gig pages + delivery samples (portfolio proof), 2+ weeks to first orders typical.
- **Fees:** **20% flat seller commission** (upwork.com/tools/fiverr-fee-calculator; freelancecompare.com). Buyer pays end 5.5% + fees on <$200 orders (fiverr.com payment terms, Sep 2025).
- **Payout:** Payoneer Revenue Card (US$1 in 2 days / $3 in 2 hours) or Local Bank Transfer via Payoneer ($3). Earnings release: 14 days after order completion (standard, 7 for Top Rated). Source: fiverr.com/legal-portal/legal-terms/payment-terms-of-service
- **Escrow:** order milestone escrow + resolution center; weaker than Upwork for sellers gotchas.
- **First 90 days:** Month 1: **₦15k–75k**; Month 3: **₦75k–300k/mo** at 2-6 orders. **Verdict: worthy 2nd platform.**

### 1.3 Freelancer.com — ⚠️ usable but race-to-bottom
- **What:** Bid marketplace + contests. Nigerian construction managers already listed (e.g. Ikord, Lagos @ $30/hr — freelancer.com/hire/construction-management).
- **Fees:** up to 10% project fee (contests up to 10% fee on winnings); membership tiers ($) reduce fee; lowball bidding culture is fierce.
- **Payout:** Payoneer withdrawals (official support pages), Skrill, PayPal (NG send-only caveat), direct bank for some regions.
- **Escrow:** milestone escrow exists; dispute service.
- **Entry barrier:** low. First 90 days: similar or worse than Upwork for NG construction skills: **₦0–150k first month** realistic. **Verdict: mid-priority, fine as a 3rd bid engine.** Source: freelancer.com/support/freelancer/payments/payoneer-withdrawals

### 1.4 PeoplePerHour — ⚠️ EU/UK-focused, slow onboarding for NG
- **What:** UK-based marketplace; strong for PM/consulting gigs; "Offers" not open bidding.
- **Fees:** ~20% earned per client up to £250 lifetime then 7.5% (approx., platform docs vary — **unverified** exact bands). Withdrawal methods: bank account, PayPal, Payoneer (peopleperhour.com/static/manual).
- **Clearing period:** new earnings can be held 1-30 days depending on 2-month history; invoice clocks: buyer pays within 7-15 days (peopleperhour.com/discover/news/freelancer-payments-process).
- **NG payout reality:** no waythrough of bank withdrawal to NGN without Payoneer/PayPal detour; PayPal NG receive restricted → Payoneer route mandatory.
- **Realistic 90 days:** **₦0–150k total** — European clients are slow to trust new, non-credited NG profiles. **Verdict: low priority; nice as 4th option only.**

### 1.5 Contra — ✅ commission-free, ideal for already-acquired clients + portfolio
- **What:** commission-free network (clients pay: $29 one-off contract or $29/mo ongoing + Stripe fee — ruul.io).
- **NG payout:** direct deposit to your USD account (Cleva/Grey/USDC) or PayPal/USDC8. 0% freelancer commission (contra.com/commission-free; getcleva.com/blog/how-to-join-contra).
- **Entry:** free; invite/portfolio to get visibility; no proposal binge-queue — you pitch clients directly on network.
- **Escrow:** not escrow-based; invoicing via Stripe; client pays commission.
- **Realistic:** ₦0 unless you bring clients (from LinkedIn/Upwork overflow/referrals). Good buffer to push off-platform clients through a legal invoice rail. **Verdict: pragmatic 4th; set up now, don't expect inbound month 1.**

### 1.6 Toptal — ⏭️ aspirational only (do NOT anchor income on it)
- **What:** top-3% screening (<3% accepted; project managers screened including construction PM experience). Client-pays rates $150–$250/hr, but freelancer rates for PMs typically $40–$120/hr (gaper.io/toptal-review, unverified).
- **NG onboarding:** possible; multi-round screening (English, live skills, portfolio defense) takes 2–5 weeks; NG payout via bank/Payoneer OK.
- **Realistic:** first-90-day income ≈ **₦0**. Apply *after* 6-12 months of Upwork track record + fire a PM portfolio. **Verdict: bookmark, revisit in 12 months.**

## 2. Nigeria-specific job boards (salary in NGN — employment, not gig)

### 2.1 Jobberman — ✅ #1 NG board; construction jobs are live
- **What:** Nigeria's largest free job board (free for candidates).
- **Construction reality (live data):** ~**300+ construction vacancies**; typical advertised pay **NGN 150k–250k/mo**, senior roles **NGN 400k+**; MEP/project engineer roles 250k–400k; AutoCAD/HND commonly required (jobberman.com/jobs/construction). Categories include Building & Architecture, Estate Agents & Property Management, **Health & Safety**, Product & Project Management.
- **Fees:** ₦0 for jobseekers. **Protection:** none (employer pays); verify company before paying anything — job-placement scams exist.
- **Timeline/income:** 2–6 weeks from application to offer; **site-supervisor-type roles ₦200k–400k/mo** → first month if landed: **₦200k–400k**.
- **Verdict: core channel. Apply 20-30 tailored apps/week; your Rexos history + HSE flags lands interviews.**

### 2.2 MyJobMag — ✅ strong construction/HSE listing flow
- **What:** free aggregator; very active construction employers (Mshel Homes — Quantity Surveyor, HSE Officer, Inventory Supervisor; Jules Construction; Megastar; Carl Inn Hotel canada- (myjobmag.com).
- **Fees:** none. **Timeline:** same 2-6 weeks. Typical Nyday 150k–400k for FM/site roles.
- **Verdict: set alerts for "site supervisor / facility / HSE / property manager".**

### 2.3 Hot Nigerian Jobs — ✅ good direct (company-run) postings
- **What:** hotnigerianjobs.com — company HR postings; recently featured *International Facilities Services (IFS) Group — facilities-management PM roles* and *Project Manager (construction company)* — directly your lane.
- **Fees:** none. **Timeline:** 1–6 weeks.
- **Verdict: check daily; FM/property pipeline listed repeatedly.**

### 2.4 Jobzilla — ✅ free daily digest + **visa-sponsorship abroad** angle
- **What:** jobzilla.ng — daily NG vacancies, CV/cover templates; also lists **visa-sponsor jobs abroad** (relevant for construction supervisors targeting GCC/UK rail).
- **Fees:** none. **Timeline:** same as other boards. **Verdict: middle channel; the visa-sponsor filter is a bonus for your trade profile.**

### 2.5 Fuzu — ✅ decent smaller board
- **What:** fuzu.com/nigeria — ~575 live vacancies; explicit categories Engineering/Architecture, Project & Program Management; free; AI CV matching. **Verdict: complementary.**

*(Honorable mentions, no rows needed: Indeed NG (~40+ site-supervisor roles at ₦200k; ng.indeed.com), LinkedIn Jobs (~30 site supervisor / ~385 site manager jobs; ng.linkedin.com) — blunt the two main aggregators via saved searches.)*

## 3. Nigeria gig / microtask & remote-Africa platforms

### 3.1 NiYA Gigs — ✅ government-backed Nigerian freelance marketplace (NGN, escrow)
- **What:** gigs.niya.gov.ng — under the Federal Ministry of Youth Development (NiYA Ecosystem), escrow-funded jobs, NGN payouts to local bank; 50k+ registered freelancers; 100+ jobs (self-reported counters).
- **Relevance:** categories include **Project Management**, Business consulting; mostly tech/creative/writing demand — construction/FM demand thin so far.
- **Fees:** platform commit provided, not disclosed on landing page (**unverified**); escrow + dispute mediation + verified IDs (documented on FAQ).
- **Realistic:** treats median gig wages ₦5k–₧100k/job; first-90-days extra **₧50k–200k** if you bid PM/ops gigs. **Verdict: unique insider advantage; register free in 15 min, use as an NGN rails-proof portfolio.**

### 3.2 GoJelly (and micro-task apps) — ⚠️ naira bridge, not a career lane
- **What:** NG micro-task marketplace; tasks ₦50–₦2,000 each; claims ₦15k–₧80k/month for active users (gojelly.com.ng, **self-reported**).
- **Entry:** zero-skill; phone-only. **Payout:** instant NGN wallet→bank.
- **Fit:** a stop-gap while training your Upwork niche; do NOT center on it — it doesn't leverage supervision skills. **Verdict: optional side-income, verify payouts with small withdrawals first (many NG microtask apps have collapsed).**

### 3.3 RemoteAfrica — ✅ clean remote-job board for Africans
- **What:** remoteafrica.io — direct-posted remote roles (no sign-in walls); employers pay $199/post; free candidate access; $5/mo "Hunter" AI-matched digest.
- **Expectation:** occasional PM/FM/ops remote stints; salary paid in USD/NGN per employer. Analytics: most roles tech/ops. **Verdict: weekly-check board, low volume for your niche.**

### 3.4 Crossover — ✅ high-USD full-time-remote pipeline (not gig, but best USD employer rail)
- **What:** crossover.com/remote-jobs/nigeria — full-time, test-hire (assessments) roles; claims up to $400k/yr across roles; NG remote workers common; typical PM/TPM roles $30k–$90k/yr → **₧3.75m–11m/yr** equivalent.
- **Pay:** paid by payroll via Payoneer/bank USD → NGN exit.
- **Fit:** their PM (agile, ops) tracks fit a site manager's delivery discipline; but they screen hard (Coding-alternative tests). **Verdict: apply in parallel; income 0 until an offer, then life-changing.**

### 3.5 RemoteRocketship / similar aggregators — ✅ low-effort aggregator
- **What:** remoterocketship.com/country/nigeria — ~1,900 remote openings incl. NG; median salary figures ($115k) are inflated aggregate — treat as noise. Free filtered alerts. **Verdict: filler channel, 10 min/week.**

*(Skipped / red flags: "RemoteWorkNG.com" — no evidence of an active, significant platform under this name (**unverified**); "Paga gig" — no active gig marketplace found (**unverified**); NaijaJobs legacy domains largely inactive → replace with Fuzu + LinkedIn; Paystack and Flutterwave are merchant rails, not freelancer payout rails.)*

## 4. The realistic first-90-days plan for THIS profile

Two-track monetization (do both):

**Track A — NGN salary rail (fastest first-NGN):**
Jobberman + MyJobMag + HotNigerianJobs + Jobzilla, 20–30 tailored applications/week for:
- Site Supervisor / Finishes Supervisor / Site Manager — advertised **₧200k–400k/mo**.
- Facilities Officer/Manager — ₧250k–500k/mo (senior at IFS-scale firms).
- HSE Officer (construction) — ₧200k–400k.
Timeline realistic: first offer 2–6 weeks; **month-1 income ₧200k–400k** if you land one.

**Track B — USD freelance (slow, highest ceiling):**
- Week 1-2: set up Payoneer + Cleva USD + NGN bank; complete Upwork profile (Construction PM / HSE documentation / Property supervision), Fiverr gigs (3), archive portfolio with Rexos photos/reports.
- Weeks 3-12: 10–15 Upwork proposals/week + Fiverr orders; first orders realistically $50–200 → **₣75k–300k by month 3**.
- Direct-service niche (underexploited): **site-supervision-as-a-service for diaspora homeowners** (photo/video progress reports, material verification, HSE checks) — sellable on WhatsApp/LinkedIn and Offshore via Upwork "virtual site supervision" tags; package at ₦50–150k/client/month — this is the highest-margin gig a real site supervisor can sell that nobody else can.

**First-month realistic total: ₣250k–600k** (Track A job + partial Track B). Pure-freelance-only month 1: **₦50k–200k**. Do not believe "₦3m by week 2" claims — slow-burn is normal; the Nigeria gig economy is valued at ~$5.18bn but 90% of that is ride-hailing/delivery, not remote services (April 2026 reports, unverified).
