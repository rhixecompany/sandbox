## 3. Working NG-native field-task apps (cross-ref; field side)

### 🇳🇬 SagaPoll — the store-visit king; deep-dive in surveys doc ❤(cross-ref): `ngn_survey_microtask_platforms.md` §4

- **What:** African survey app + **Store Visits** (photo + barcode-scan local shops — your supervisor instinct is the literal skill set).
- **Pay:** 5,000 pts = **$20 ≈ ₦30,000** (SagaPoll FB, 2026; Play listing). Reviewer: 5,000 pts "in 2 days" via store visits (Google Play 3.9★/15k).
- **Cities:** nationwide; concentrated where merchants modernize aisles (Lagos metro, Abuja, Port Harcourt).
- **Payout:** bank transfer, mobile money, or airtime at 5,000 pts; bank takes days but lands (Play reviews).
- **Reimbursement:** none needed (audit-type, not purchases).

### 📍 Premise — location/photo tasks (cross-ref)

- **Pay:** home-photo/location tasks ≈ ₦150–2,000; **NGN top-up min ≈ ₦1,800; BTC min ≈ ₦16,000** (NG walkthrough, YouTube 2025; MWM review).
- **Status:** active in NG (no VPN). Task supply patchy; review delays on photo tasks.

### 📱 GeoPoll — SMS/web field surveys → airtime

- **Average**: ≈$0.50 airtime / survey (geopoll.com/community NG endorsements); occasional geo-location field projects. Rail: airtime auto, no PayPal NG.
