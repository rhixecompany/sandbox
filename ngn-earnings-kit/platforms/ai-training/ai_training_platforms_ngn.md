# AI Training / Data Annotation / LLM Evaluation Platforms for Nigeria (NGN Earnings Kit)

**Currency baseline:** US$1 ≈ ₦1,500 (mid-2026). All USD rates converted to NGN at this rate. **Everything is reported in original currency + NGN equivalent — never in GBP/EUR.**
**Scope:** Platforms that accept (or likely accept) workers based in Nigeria and pay **better than Outlier**. Outlier baseline for Nigerians (see §0): ₦15,000–₦75,000/hr ($10–50/hr), weekly via PayPal/Airtm/ACH.
**Method:** 12 web searches + 4 full-page extractions (RemoWork, MyJobMag, eCourseware.ng, aitrainingjobs.it) plus platform/support pages. Every pay figure is sourced; unverified items are flagged. Nothing here is financial or tax advice.

## 0. Baseline: Outlier (the platform you're comparing against)

**NGN verified? ✅** — Nigerians actively work it without VPN (TechPoint 30-day account by a Nigerian writer, 2026); Nigerian Facebook/YouTube groups discuss $18–$50/hr offers. Note some Nigerian users historically reported location filtering (and resorted to Airtm) — verify you can onboard with your NG address.

- **Pay:** $10–50/hr typical; math/coding $15–60; text/editing $10–15 (RemoWork + TechPoint). NGN: ₦15,000–₦75,000/hr.
- **Entry:** signup + unpaid qualification tasks per subject area; weekly Tuesdays payout; PayPal/ACH/Airtm (Airtm is the key one for Nigeria).

## 2. NGN-adjusted earnings outlook (realistic)

- **Full-time, skilled (5 h/day × 6 d/wk ≈ 130 h/mo):** Mercor / Alignerr (expert) / Braintrust at $15–60 → **₦2.9M–₦11.7M/mo**.
- **Part-time, mid-tier (DA / Stellar / Mindrift specialist):** $15–25/hr → **₦700k–₦1.2M per 20 h/wk**.
- **Entry tier (CrowdGen, Toloka, Clickworker, OneForma):** $3–12/hr → **₦180k–₦700k per 20 h/wk** — task availability, not the rate, is the ceiling.

### 1. Mercor — highest officially-verified NG rate

- **NGN? ✅** — Nigeria is on Mercor's official supported-countries list (mercor.com/supported-countries and talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/supported-countries), and Nigerian users report live offers (a $77/hr "Offensive Cyber Expert" mercor@IG offer circulated Aug 2026).
- **Pay:** AI Model Specialist ~$16/hr start; typical AI roles $20–100+; expert median quoted $106/hr; engineering $70–200+.
- **NGN:** ₦24,000–₦150,000/hr typical; expert ₦160,000+/hr.
- **Entry:** 20-minute AI video interview (retakable up to 3×) + skills assessments; no invite wall. Time-tracking via Insightful — use a dedicated device.
- **Payout:** weekly; direct bank/Wise-style rails (verify in-app).
  Sources: https://www.mercor.com/supported-countries · https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/supported-countries · remowork.life/blog/top-ai-training-platforms-that-pay-you-to-train-ai-models-in-2026

## 5. Nigerian tax (FIRS / CBN) notes — keep it simple but real

1. You are a **sole trader** for tax purposes: USD income from AI training is _taxable foreign-source income_ for Nigerian residents (Finance Act 2021 widened reach; words like PITA provision 3(1)(a)-(b)).
2. Register a **TIN** (first time, free) via the FIRS portal or through your bank (select "self-employed"), file your **personal income tax return & annual Self-Assessment before 30 June** each year. Most platforms **don't withhold** — you file on your own, and the risk is years of underreporting + penalties + FIRS garnisheeing bank accounts.
3. Keep proof: platform task logs, payout statements, local conversion records (Payoneer/Wise statements). This also helps you.
4. If you later formalize (RC registration, invoice an agency), you become responsible for **CIT/VAT** — not needed for most solos.
5. On the money movement side: receiving via PayPal/Payoneer/Airtm/Wise is legal (CBN circulars), but **CBN requested FX operational compliance for NGN flows from remittances — keep details of the source**.
6. Never fake paying-country (VPN/address) to unlock higher regional rates — you'd create false tax/residency statements and illegally use another tax jurisdiction.

## 6. 3-week starting plan (recommended order)

1. **Week 1 — apply & test:** Mercor + DataAnnotation + Stellar (all free). Do their unpaid assessments thoroughly — your rate bracket is set by assessment quality.
2. **Week 2 — stack stable-USD:** OneForma/CrowdGen/TELUS (lower rates, steadier), Invisible jobs already visible.
3. **Week 3 — specialize:** If you have a degree or domain edge (construction/facilities ops → logistics/HSQ experts are in demand on Alignerr/Micro1/Braintrust), translate your **10 yrs site-supervision experience into "AI agent task author (operations & logistics)"** roles — those pay $40–120/hr and NG can qualify via contracts rather than location.
4. Don't pay for "inside tips", "accounts", or "priority queues" — every legit platform charges you nothing.
