# NGN Cashback, Receipt-Scanning & Rewards Apps — Nigeria (mid-2026)

**Profile:** Site Supervisor, monthly spend basket ≈ ₦60,000–₦150,000 (airtime+data ~₦15k, electricity ~₦20k, supermarket/groceries ~₦40k, misc bills/card spend ~₦25k).
**FX anchor:** US$1 ≈ ₦1,500.
**Verdict up front:** There is **no viable international receipt-scanning app in Nigeria** (Fetch, Magic Receipts, Ibotta, Receipt Hog are all US/Canada/UK-only). The *real* NGN cashback plays are **local**: fintech bill-payment cashback (OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint, JumiaPay, Kuda), cheap-data/airtime reseller apps, and telco loyalty (MTN/Airtel/Glo). Total realistic stack: **₦2,500–₦6,000/month (~$2–$4)** for the basket above — a small but real % return, not a side income. Entries marked (unverified) are claims seen on social/third-party pages not confirmed on official sites.

## 1) LOCAL NIGERIAN CASHBACK / REWARD APPS (viable)

### 1.1 OPay — cashback on airtime/data/bills

- **How it works:** Fintech super-app (CBN-licensed, NDIC-insured). "OPay Cashback" loyalty program credits cashback to wallet when you buy airtime/data and pay bills through the app; also daily interest on savings and free transfers.
- **Realistic NGN benefit:** Up to **6% cashback on airtime & data** per official Google Play listing. At ₦15,000/mo airtime+data → **₦300–₦900/mo** (promo-dependent; take 2–4% mid-range).
- **Payout / minimum:** Cashback lands in OPay wallet instantly; spend or transfer to bank. No real minimum to receive; wallet funds cash out via bank transfer.
- **Signup bonus:** Periodic (e.g. first top-up bonuses, daily check-in rewards) — not fixed.
- **NGN availability:** **Verified** — Nigeria core market, CBN-licensed. (Google Play team.opay.pay; opayweb.com)
- **Red flags:** Cashback % drops when promos end; card at issuers/MFB risk notes; some user reports of account freezes on suspicious patterns. Low risk overall (regulated).
- Sources: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=team.opay.pay (NG listing, "up to 6%"); https://www.opayweb.com/terms-and-conditions; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nigeria-loyalty-programs-market-databook-141000965.html (Opa Pet cashback confirmed in NG market data)

### 1.2 PalmPay — cashback + PalmPoints

- **How it works:** Digital bank + rewards. Every airtime/data/bill purchase earns cashback % (score tiered by monthly airtime transactions) plus **PalmPoints** which can discount up to **50% of a transaction**.
- **Realistic NGN:** Historical promos said "up to 15–20%" airtime (2019-2023 era); current normal tiers ~1–5%. ₦15,000/mo airtime+data → **₦400–₦1,200/mo** incl. PalmPoints discounts. Points cap advertised historically ₦10,000 value/mo (unverified current).
- **Payout:** PalmPoints/cashback auto-credited; cashback stays in wallet, convertible/deductible at payment. Free transfers out.
- **Minimum payout:** none for cashback; PalmPoints redeemable at checkout (up to 50% discount per PalmPay).
- **Signup:** formerly up to ₦100 free for new users (promo, historical) (unverified).
- **NGN availability:** **Verified** (Nigeria core market, CBN-licensed).
- **Red flags:** Cashback tiers fluctuate with promos; some users report points not crediting for certain bills; aggressive promo messaging. Entry-level "free ₦100" attached to referrals.
- Sources: https://x.com/palmpay_ng/status/1499784497323298816 (PalmPoints up to 50% discount); https://www.instagram.com/p/CFmER_WDJTc (up to 15% airtime + palm points, old); https://www.palmpay.com; https://www.facebook.com/palmpayapp.ng (₦10,000 PalmPoints/mo, old)

### 1.3 Moniepoint (personal app) — Rewards

- **How it works:** Moniepoint MFB app — "Moniepoint Rewards": cashback on every airtime/data recharge (official Play Store: "Recharge and enjoy up to 2% cashback"), plus discounts; merchant POS tier program runs separately (July–Sep 2025 promo: up to **15% back on POS fees** and **up to ₦6 per interbank transfer** — merchant side, not consumer).
- **Realistic NGN:** ₦15,000 airtime+data → **₦150–₦300/mo** consumer-side. (Merchant POS = bigger but not in scope.)
- **Payout:** cashback/wallet balance.
- **NGN availability:** Verified (CBN-licensed MFB, Nigeria-only).
- **Red flags:** POS merchant cashback was a _promotion_ (16 Jul–16 Sep 2025) — do not model as permanent; personal app % is low.
- Sources: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.moniepoint.personal (up to 2% on Airtime); https://moniepoint.com/blog/pos-transaction-cashback-rewards (tiers, ₦6/transfer, 15% fees)

### 1.4 Kuda — Kuda Coins + Premium

- **How it works:** Digital bank (CBN-licensed). Earn **Kuda Coins** on transactions (≥₦1,000, Kuda Plus) → 50 Coins unlock **Kuda Premium**: "Earn cashback on bills" + brand discounts, 25 free transfers/month. Kuda Business: 1.5% cashback on all airtime + bill payments.
- **Realistic NGN:** Personal Premium bill cashback in small promo percentages; electricity/airtime on Kuda ~ ₦15-20k of spend → **₦150–₦400/mo** (mostly promo-driven). Business account 1.5% is the clearer number if she controls any business payments.
- **Payout:** cashback credits to account; NGN wallet; zero-cost transfers.
- **NGN availability:** Verified (Nigeria; Coins require Tier 3 account + ₦50k avg balance for Premium).
- **Red flags:** Premium requires avg balance ₦50,000; reward sizes are modest; perks change frequently.
- Sources: https://www.kuda.com (Premium — cashback on bills; 4.8★), https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kuda-free-transfer-payment/id1467373738 (50 Coins, Tier 3), https://sme.kuda.com/ng/business/cashback-airtime-bills/ (Business 1.5%)

## 3) TELCO LOYALTY (airtime/data rewards — all verified for NG)

### 3.1 MTN

- **How it works:** (a) **MTN Pulse** (youth tariff): dial `*406*1#` → join, accrue points redeemable for free airtime/data; (b) **myMTN NG app** — "Bundle Loyalty Reward" (in official release notes) + channel discounts; (c) **MTN Prestige** — for premium customers (avg spend ~₦5k+/month): birthday/anniversary free data, surcharge-discounted bundles, VIP perks; (d) periodic cash-prise loyalty campaigns.
- **Realistic NGN:** For a ₦10k+/mo MTN user: **₦0–₦500/mo** equivalent (points/discount data), Prestige tier fastest.
- **NGN availability:** Verified (myMTN app official; Prestige official MTN page).
- **Red flags:** Prestige invites/spend bands change; loyalty data often expires.
- Sources: https://www.mtn.ng/personal/prestige; https://www.mtn.ng/helppersonal/mtn-prestige (spend bands); web myMTN NG release notes; https://www.telecompaper.com/news/mtn-nigeria-launches-cash-giveaway--1541242

### 3.2 Airtel

- **How it works:** (a) **My Airtel App** — "users earn bonuses on recharge and data purchases" (official page); (b) **Airtel Cash Token Rewards** promos (dial *479# opt-in; *6700*amount# discount codes; every purchase enters draws).
- **Realistic NGN:** bonuses = promo value ~**₦0–₦200/mo**; token draws lottery.
- **NGN availability:** Verified (official Airtel NG pages).
- **Sources:** https://www.airtel.com.ng/ng/personal/my-airtel-app; https://www.facebook.com/airtelng/posts/4342333362507911

### 3.3 Glo

- **Realistic NGN:** ₦1,000/wk → ~₦32/month CashToken value + lottery odds.

- **Red flags:** CashToken value is tiny; draws lotto odds; program runs in seasons.
- **Sources:** https://www.gloworld.com/ng/blog/posts/subscribers-to-enjoy-another-season-of-glo-rewards-cashtoken; https://flex.flexible: https://cashtokenreward.com/2024/04/02/how-to-get-cashtoken-reward-from-glo; https://www.facebook.com/GloWorld/posts/10157660204383435

### 4.5 Swagbucks full platform — **marginal**

If everlive, payout floor 500 SB (₦~₦7,500 notional?) but **gift cards are US/UK-centric and there's no NGN payout**; realistic yield for NG users is survey-quota ~₦0–₦750/mo. ## 6) Rules of engagement

1. Only use **CBN-licensed wallets** for money storage (Kuda, OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint) — cashback apps can vanish; withdraw weekly.
2. Never pay to join; never borrow for cashback; never use "double your money" bill apps.
3. Run all daily-mob recharges through 1–2 chosen apps (volume → tier): OPay/PalmPay/JumiaPay.
4. Redeem Glo/Airtel/Mtn points the same month they're earned (seasons rotate).
5. Re-verify percentages quarterly — NG promos rotate hard.
