## 1. Honeygain — Bandwidth Sharing

- **What:** Sells your idle bandwidth to web-intelligence clients. Runs in background on Android/PC.
- **NG availability:** ✅ **VERIFIED.** Nigeria is on Honeygain's official Tipalti/PayPal payout country list (support.honeygain.com, "33 Nigeria"). Base bandwidth-sharing mode operates in 100+ countries incl. NG; the bonus **Content Delivery mode is NOT available in Nigeria** (Africa list = South Africa only). (Official help center, 2026)
- **Realistic ₦N/month:** $1–3/device/month; $3–10 with 3–5 devices → **₦4,500–15,000**. With Content Delivery excluded in NG and low NG IP demand, expect the LOW end: **₦2,000–5,000 first months**.
- **Battery/data:** Low battery drain; on WiFi uses idle bandwidth only. **On metered data it consumes your bundle — net loss** (data ≈ $0.39/GB in NG vs $0.10/GB earned).
- **Privacy:** MED. Client traffic flows through your IP address; you can't see what's routed. You can be associated with client activity. No malware historically, but proxy-network exposure is real.
- **Payout:** PayPal (through Tipalti) or JumpToken (crypto). **Min payout $20 (20,000 credits)** — the single biggest friction point: on 1 device expect **4–8 months to first payout** (independently verified: real user took 5 months; $17.96 net after fees).
- **Verdict:** 🟢 **LEGIT** — >1M completed payouts, Tipalti 2025 Mass Payments award, ~48h processing, 30+ years of track record... (unverified) _actually: established since 2020_. Small but real. **Only worth starting if you have a PC/old phone to leave on 24/7 + unlimited WiFi.**

## 🚨 3 worst scam patterns to avoid

1. **Cloud-mining / "hash power" apps promising guaranteed daily returns** ("earn 1–5% daily", fixed APY, rent hash power). $500M+ lost to these in 2024; fake dashboards + Ponzi payments; Nigeria-specific clones everywhere. If it asks you to deposit before you "withdraw," it is a scam. **100% avoid.**
2. **Brand-new "attention/donation" apps with no footprint** — e.g., Spilr-distributed-content style, DistributedMint-style "donate your data" — no Play Store presence, no independent reviews, only TikTok/Telegram promoters, pay in "points" or private tokens with a fixed withdrawal wall that never opens. No company, no payout proof = do not install.
3. **Any "passive" scheme that requires a fee or asset transfer to start/withdraw:** "activation fee," "mining pool top-up," "migration," "verification deposit," agency registration, paying for "upgraded accounts" (ByteBenefit-style upgrade upsells included). Popularized in NG by fake "data-selling" and "sponsored" app-network 2026 slides → they're advance-fee scams nearly always. Legit bandwidth apps are free to join and free to withdraw.
