# 🇳🇬 Tax Guidance — Nigeria 2026 (FIRS)

**For Alexa — side-earnings from AI training, surveys, user testing, cashback, etc.**

> Last verified: 2026-08-08 · Sources cited below. Tax law is fluid — re-verify before filing.

## 1. Do I even owe tax?

Yes — Nigeria taxes residents on **worldwide income**, including online/remote side earnings. BUT the **Nigeria Tax Act 2025** (in force **1 January 2026**) makes the first **₦800,000 of annual income tax-free** for every individual — employee, freelancer, or self-employed.

- If your TOTAL annual income (all sources) stays **≤ ₦800,000** → no PIT payable.
- Above that, progressive bands apply (see table).
- The old "minimum tax" 1% rule is gone. The ₦70,000 minimum wage is fully exempt.

## 2. Applicable bands (Nigeria Tax Act 2025)

| Annual taxable income (NGN) | Rate |
| --------------------------- | ---- |
| First 800,000               | 0%   |
| Next 2,200,000              | 15%  |
| Next 9,000,000              | 18%  |
| Next 13,000,000             | 21%  |
| Next 25,000,000             | 23%  |
| Above 50,000,000            | 25%  |

Source: PWC Tax Summaries — Nigeria (reviewed 29 May 2026): https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nigeria/individual/taxes-on-personal-income

## 3. Do you need to register / self-assess?

- **Self-assessment is mandatory for anyone with taxable income above the threshold** — if you're a freelancer/remote worker, expect to file.
- Register for a **Tax Identification Number (TIN)** via FIRS: https://www.firs.gov.ng/ (also state IRS / JTB — Tax ID is now auto-issued for individuals).
- Keep business records — you'll file a self assessment return for the year.

## 4. What's deductible (expenses you can offset against side income)

- Internet / data for work
- Equipment (laptop/phone) — depreciation or outright where allowable
- Electricity / work-from-home costs (reasonable portion)
- Professional services (accountant/tax consultant)
- Rent relief: 20% of annual rent up to ₦500,000 (NTA 2025)
- Pension (PFA), NHIS/NHIA, NHF contributions
- Life insurance / annuity premiums
- Interest on loans for owner-occupied housing

Keep receipts and bank statements ≥6 years.

## 5. Foreign platform specifics

- Platforms (Outlier, Atapoll, user testing etc.) are foreign; they **usually do not** withhold Nigerian income tax. You're responsible.
- Payouts in USD → convert to NGN at CBN/I&E rate on the date received, keep the receipt.
- PayPal/Payoneer fees are real cash expenses — deductible (keep statements).

## 7. Scam alert (tax)

- FIRS never demands payment in "gift cards" / crypto; never share BVN, OTP, or NIN on a video call "to update tax records".
- Legit FIRS contacts: official website/number; any agent demanding instant "clearance fee" = red flag.
