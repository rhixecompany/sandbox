# ☠️ Scam Warnings — NGN Earnings Kit

**Golden rule: you should NEVER pay to earn. No activation fee, no "refundable deposit", no card-before-signup.**

## Red Flags (auto-eliminate)

- ❌ Upfront payment / "registration fee" / "activation fee"
- ❌ Guarantee of huge daily returns with no work
- ❌ Asks for BVN + OTP / card PIN / seed phrase
- ❌ Telegram/WhatsApp-only "task" schemes (very common in NG: "taskers" paying for likes/orders then demand deposit)
- ❌ Asks you to verify with a payment before "unlocking" withdrawals
- ❌ Vague company, no address, no registration (CAC check)
- ❌ Bonus requires you to refer N people before ANY withdrawal
- ❌ Platform unreachable / support ghost after signup

## Known scam patterns in Nigeria (2026)

- WhatsApp "Telegram mini-task" scams (e.g., "Social Media Task" paying USDT then freeze)
- "AI annotation training" job scams — recruiter charges "training fee"
- Fake "bank switch" / "loan recovery" apps collecting secrets
- Phishing sites that clone Outlier/CrowdGen login pages (check the exact domain)

## Verification checklist before joining

- [ ] Domain registered >1yr (use whois / Cloudflare radar)
- [ ] Real company "About" page with address & registration ID
- [ ] Payment method you can't be reverse-scammed (bank transfer reversible; crypto NOT reversible)
- [ ] Real reviews on Trustpilot / Reddit (independent — not the app's store page)
- [ ] No "instant withdraw guaranteed" magic

## The "too good to be true" rate filter

If a survey pays ₦5,000/hour or an AI task pays $40/hr for easy work → verify with 3 independent sources first (unverified).
