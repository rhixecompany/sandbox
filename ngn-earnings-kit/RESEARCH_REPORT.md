## 5. Tax & compliance (critical for NG)
- Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (in force 1 Jan 2026): **₦800,000/yr tax-free threshold** for all individuals.
- PIT bands: ₦800k-3M @15%, 3-12M @18%, 12-25M @21%, 25-50M @23%, >50M @25%.
- Self-assessment mandatory above threshold; TIN auto-issued; foreign platforms usually don't withhold — you report.
- Income to record in NGN (CBN/I&E rate), expenses deductible (internet/data/equipment/rent relief 20% up to ₦500k).
