# 🤝 Sample Referral Messages — NGN

Customize & send. Don't be spammy — referral invites fail when they feel automated.

## To a friend (WhatsApp)
> Hey! I've been earning a bit with [Platform] — honest work from home, pays into my bank in naira. If you want the ₦[X] signup bonus, my referral link: [link]. No fee, no catch — you just need to complete one task to get [bonus]. Happy to walk you through it.

## To a colleague (telegram)
> Bro, I found a legit side-hustle — [Platform] pays [rate]. Referral link below if you want the free bonus. They pay [payout method]. Won't spam you more, just sharing because it worked for me.

## To an online community (Reddit / WhatsApp group) — when sharing a real find
```
I've tested this one personally — [Platform], pays ~₦X per task, pays out to [method] in about [days]. Signup link (referral, get a bonus): [link]. Tips: complete the intro screener carefully, expect [X%] disqualification when surveys are scarce. Not sponsored — just my experience.
```

## Rules
- Always disclose the referral.
- Give real experience, not hype.
- Nothing that looks like multi-level marketing. Referral = one-time bonus.
