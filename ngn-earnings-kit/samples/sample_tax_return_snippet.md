# 🧾 Sample Tax Return Snippet — NGN

Illustrative only — verify with FIRS & a professional at filing time. Shows the _shape_ of self-assessment records under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025.

## Sample annual summary (single person, side income)

| Item                                                                  | NGN                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Income A (AI training, mixed FX→NGN)                                  | 610,000                  |
| Income B (surveys)                                                    | 210,000                  |
| Less: allowable expenses (internet ₦120k, data ₦40k, equipment ₦180k) | (340,000)                |
| **Net taxable income**                                                | **480,000**              |
| Less: tax-free threshold (first ₦800,000)                             | (480,000)                |
| **Tax payable estimate**                                              | **₦0** (below threshold) |

## Second example — crossing the threshold

- Gross side income: ₦1,100,000
- Allowable expenses: (₦200,000)
- **Net taxable income: ₦900,000**
- 0% on first ₦800,000 → remainder ₦100,000 @ 15% = **₦15,000** (illustrative)

## Key reminders

- Convert foreign income at the CBN/I&E rate on the date of receipt.
- Keep proof of every expense (receipts/utility bills/online invoices).
- The ₦800,000 exemption is per-person under NTA 2025; report worldwide income if resident.
- Self-employment / remote income is taxable under the graduated bands — register on FIRS portal (JTB) and file annual self assessment when above threshold.
- Minimum-wage employees (≤ ₦70k/mo) may be fully exempt from PAYE deductions.

## Structure of a simple sole-trader record

```
Date | Gross | FX rate | NGN gross | Expenses | Net | Remarks
```
