## 2. DataAnnotation.tech — Qualification Test Tips

**Format:** 1-3 hour unpaid assessment (10-20% pass rate)

**What they test:**
- **Writing tasks:** You're given a prompt, write a response. Show reasoning, use examples, be thorough.
- **Ranking tasks:** Compare 2-4 AI responses. Explain WHY one is better (facts, clarity, safety, completeness).
- **Code tasks (if applicable):** Debugging, writing functions, explaining logic.

**Sample writing task response:**

```
Prompt: "Explain how compound interest works to a teenager"

Response: Imagine you put £100 in a savings account that pays 10% interest every year.
Year 1: You earn £10 interest. Total = £110.
Year 2: You earn 10% of £110 = £11 interest. Total = £121.
Year 3: You earn 10% of £121 = £12.10. Total = £133.10.

See how the interest amount grows each year? That's because you're earning
interest ON your interest — not just on your original £100. That's compound
interest. The longer you leave it, the faster it grows.

Key points to include: initial principal, interest rate, compounding frequency,
time horizon. Use concrete numbers, relatable example, step-by-step breakdown.
```

**Tips:**
- Write more than the minimum — thorough answers score higher
- Show your reasoning step-by-step (chain of thought)
- If ranking, justify each score with specific examples from the text
- Grammar and spelling matter — proofread before submitting
- If you fail, you can retry after 6 months with a different email

## 3. Mercor — AI Interview Prep (Zara)

**Format:** Automated AI interviewer (Zara), recorded video/audio answers
**Time:** ~30-45 minutes
**Topics:** Background, problem-solving, written English assessment

**Sample questions + how to answer:**

```
Q: "Tell me about yourself"
A: I'm based in the UK with a background in [your field]. I'm detail-oriented
and enjoy tasks that require careful analysis and clear communication.
I've been looking for flexible remote work and AI training caught my interest
because it uses those skills directly.

Q: "Describe a time you solved a difficult problem"
A: [Use STAR method — Situation, Task, Action, Result]
Situation: I was managing [project/task] and encountered [problem].
Task: I needed to [goal] within [constraint].
Action: I broke the problem down into [steps], researched [approach],
and implemented [solution].
Result: [Outcome — metric if possible, e.g., "completed 2 days early"]
```

**Tips:**
- Speak clearly, at a moderate pace
- Look at the camera, not the screen
- Have good lighting and a quiet room
- Use specific examples, not generic statements
- If you stumble, pause and restart your answer

## 4. General Application — Writing Sample

Many platforms ask for a writing sample. Use this template:

```
Topic: Explain [concept] to a [beginner/expert/child]

[Your response here — 3-5 paragraphs]

Key elements to include:
1. Define the concept in simple terms first
2. Provide a concrete example
3. Explain why it matters / real-world application
4. Address common misconceptions
5. End with a summary or key takeaway
```

## 5. Domain Expert Application (Finance, Law, Medicine, Coding)

If applying for specialist roles, structure your bio like this:

```
Domain Expertise: [Finance / Law / Medicine / Software Engineering]
Experience: [X] years in [specific area]
Qualifications: [Relevant certifications, degrees]

I can evaluate AI outputs in [domain] for:
- Technical accuracy — is the content factually correct?
- Logical consistency — does the reasoning hold up?
- Regulatory compliance — does it follow UK/EU [regulations]?
- Practical applicability — would this advice work in the real world?

Sample strengths:
- [Specific skill 1, e.g., "UK tax law knowledge"]
- [Specific skill 2, e.g., "Python debugging and code review"]
- [Specific skill 3, e.g., "Medical terminology and clinical reasoning"]
```

## 6. OpenTrain AI / OneForma — Basic Application

Less competitive, simpler applications. Key tips:

- Complete your profile 100% — incomplete profiles get fewer tasks
- Take the onboarding training seriously (quality score affects task allocation)
- Start with lower-paying tasks to build your quality score
- Once you hit 4.5/5+ quality, apply for higher-paying projects
- Check for new tasks daily — good ones go fast
