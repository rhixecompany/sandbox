## Trading Allowance (£1,000)

If your total side earnings are **under £1,000** per tax year, you don't need to register with HMRC or pay tax. No expenses needed — the allowance covers it automatically.

If you earn **over £1,000**, you can either:

1. **Use the trading allowance** (£1,000 tax-free) and pay tax on anything over that
2. **Deduct actual expenses** and pay tax on the profit (may be better if expenses > £1,000)

**Choose whichever gives the lower taxable profit.**
