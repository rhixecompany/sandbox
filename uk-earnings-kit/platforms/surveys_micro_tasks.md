# Survey & Micro-Task Platforms — Deep Dive

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 | **Category:** Surveys

## Overview

Survey and micro-task platforms pay users to complete online surveys, small tasks, game offers, and app testing. While individual studies pay less (£0.50-3 each), stacking multiple platforms creates a reliable income stream. Prolific is the clear leader in this category for UK users.

## Recommended Stack

### Primary (Daily Driver)

1. **Prolific** — Morning/evening sessions, £20-30/week for 2 hrs/day
2. **Freecash** — Game offers when available, £5-15/week

### Secondary (Stack On)

1. **Qmee** — Background surveys while browsing, £5-10/week
2. **YouGov + Panelbase** — Weekly poll hits, £5-10/week combined

### Tertiary (Fill Gaps)

1. **Swagbucks** — Multi-method, good for variety
2. **Branded Surveys** — When others are quiet
3. **Ipsos iSay + Research Opinion Panel** — Reliable backup

## Prolific — Detailed

**£8-15/hr** | ✅ UK | **Verdict: ⭐ Best Survey Platform**

- **How it works:** Academic researchers post studies; you get notified when eligible
- **About You section:** Fill this out completely to maximize study invites
- **Payout:** PayPal — cashout threshold £5, paid instantly
- **Study types:** Academic surveys, psychology experiments, product research
- **UK Compatibility:** Excellent — many UK-specific studies
- **Tips:**
  - Keep Prolific Assistant browser extension installed
  - Check regularly (studies fill up fast)
  - Complete your "About You" profile thoroughly
  - Be honest — they detect inconsistent answers
- **Earnings:** £20-30/week at 2 hrs/day, £100+ possible
