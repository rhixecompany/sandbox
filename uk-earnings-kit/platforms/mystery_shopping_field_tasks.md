# Mystery Shopping & Field Tasks — Deep Dive

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 | **Category:** Field Tasks

## Overview

Mystery shopping and field task apps pay you to visit stores, take photos, check displays, or complete small tasks in-person. Great if you're already out and about — combine with grocery shopping or commuting.

## Tips for Maximising
- Enable notifications — best tasks go fast
- Combine tasks in the same area (route optimisation)
- Check apps before heading to a shopping centre
- Take clear photos (good lighting, straight angles)
- Build a good rating to unlock higher-paying tasks
