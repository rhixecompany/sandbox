# Market Research Panels — Deep Dive

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 | **Category:** Market Research

## Overview

Market research panels pay users to share opinions, test concepts, and participate in studies. These tend to pay better than standard survey panels (£5-15/hr average) with occasional high-value focus groups (£30-100).

## Strategy

- Use **Research Opinion Panel** and **Viewpoint** for weekly surveys
- Sign up for **TalkingPoint**, **Kantar**, **SIS International** for high-value focus groups
- Combine with UserInterviews/Respondent for maximum study access
- Keep demographic profiles updated to qualify for more studies
