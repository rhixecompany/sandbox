# Freelance & Gig Platforms — Deep Dive

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 | **Category:** Freelance

## Overview

Freelance platforms connect you with one-off or ongoing projects. For construction/property background, platforms like MyBuilder, Bark, and RatedPeople are ideal for site supervision, property management, and related skills.
