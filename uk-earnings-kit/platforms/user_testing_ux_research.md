# User Testing & UX Research Platforms — Deep Dive

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 | **Category:** UX Research

## Overview

User testing pays you to share your screen and talk through your experience using websites, apps, or prototypes. Tests typically last 15-20 min and pay £10-30 each. The key is qualifying for screeners — complete your demographic profile honestly and thoroughly.

## Recommended Stack

### Primary

1. **UserTesting** — Most consistent, £10/test, 1-3/week
2. **UserInterviews** — High-paying studies £50-150

### Secondary

1. **Userlytics** — Good backup, same format as UserTesting
2. **UsabilityHub** — Quick 5-min tests in downtime

### Premium

1. **Respondent** — When you qualify for high-value studies
2. **UserZoom** — Enterprise-grade studies

## Tips

- Complete ALL profile questions to qualify for more tests
- Check screeners daily (most disappear within hours)
- Use a good microphone — audio quality matters
- Speak naturally, think aloud, don't script
- Test in a quiet room with stable internet
