## Recommended Stack

### Setup (5 min)

1. **TopCashback** + **Quidco** for all online shopping
2. **Shopprize** for receipt scanning
3. **Chase UK** for 1% debit card cashback
4. **Jam Doughnut** for gift card purchases

### Maximising Cashback

- Always check TopCashback/Quidco before buying anything online
- Link loyalty cards (Nectar, Clubcard) automatically
- Scan ALL receipts in Shopprize weekly
- Use Chase debit for everyday spending
- Consider Complete Savings if you shop at 500+ retailers
