## Overview

AI training and data annotation platforms pay workers to train AI models by completing tasks like ranking responses, writing prompts, verifying accuracy, labelling data, and testing model outputs. This category offers the highest £/hr for UK workers who are skilled, reliable, and can pass qualification tests.

## Recommended Platforms — Detailed

### 1. DataAnnotation.tech

**£20-40/hr** | ✅ UK | **Verdict: ⭐ Top Pick**

- **Type:** AI training, coding, writing, reasoning tasks
- **Signup:** Take a starter assessment (30-60 min), then ongoing qualification tasks
- **Payout:** PayPal — cashed out every 7 days (no minimum after first)
- **Work:** Consistent, varied tasks — coding, creative writing, fact-checking, maths
- **Pros:** Reliable work, good pay, interesting tasks, flexible hours
- **Cons:** Initial qualification is lengthy and can be rejected; work can dry up during slow periods
- **UK Compatibility:** Full UK access, pays in USD
- **Reddit Sentiment:** Generally positive, £20-40/hr consistent
- **Trustpilot:** 4.3/5 (800+ reviews)

### 2. Mercor

**$40-150/hr** | ✅ UK | **Verdict: ⭐ Top Earner**

- **Type:** AI interview/evaluation platform
- **Signup:** 1-3 day onboarding process, AI-matched to projects
- **Payout:** Wise or bank transfer — weekly
- **Work:** AI model evaluation, domain expertise consulting
- **Pros:** Highest pay rates, fast onboarding, weekly payments
- **Cons:** Limited availability, requires specific expertise, less consistent volume
- **UK Compatibility:** Full UK access, pays in USD via Wise
- **Reddit Sentiment:** Very positive but limited to skilled professionals

### 3. Invisible Technologies

**$25-100/hr** | ✅ UK | **Verdict: ⭐ Good Backup**

- **Type:** Long-term structured AI training projects
- **Signup:** Multi-stage application process
- **Payout:** Bank transfer — bi-weekly
- **Work:** Ongoing project-based work, process-driven
- **Pros:** Long-term stability, structured workflows, good management
- **Cons:** Longer qualification process, not immediate start
- **UK Compatibility:** Full UK access

## Tax Notes

- All AI training income is taxable as self-employment income
- Use the trading allowance (£1,000) if total side earnings are under £1,000
- Most platforms pay in USD — be aware of exchange rate fluctuations
- Keep records of all payments received for HMRC
