## Strategy

1. **Keep one "burner" account** (Monzo/Starling/Chase) with 2–3 active direct debits
2. **Switch burner → best current offer** via CASS (7 working days, guaranteed)
3. **Wait for bonus** (usually 7–30 days after switch completes)
4. **Move direct debits back** to main account OR keep them on new account
5. **Repeat** with next best offer (max 3–4×/year to avoid credit file flags)
6. **Track every switch** in this log for tax (PSA) and credit file awareness

---

## Credit File Notes

- Each switch = **hard search** on credit file
- Multiple searches close together = minor score dip (recovers in 3–6 months)
- Space switches **3+ months apart** if planning mortgage/loan application
- Use **soft-search eligibility checkers** (MSE Credit Club, ClearScore) before applying

---

## CASS (Current Account Switch Service) Guarantees

- ✅ Completes in **7 working days**
- ✅ Moves **all** direct debits, standing orders, salary
- ✅ Redirects payments for **36 months** (safety net)
- ✅ **Guaranteed** — if anything goes wrong, bank refunds charges
- ✅ Free to use
