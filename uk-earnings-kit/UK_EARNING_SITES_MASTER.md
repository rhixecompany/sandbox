## Detailed Platform Breakdown

### TIER 1: HIGH £/HR (£20+)

#### 1. Matched Betting — Outplayed (formerly OddsMonkey) / OddsMonkey

- **What:** Risk-free betting using bookmaker free bets + lay bets on exchanges
- **Earnings:** £400–500 first month (welcome offers), £100–300/month ongoing (reloads)
- **Cost:** £15–30/month subscription
- **Tax:** **Tax-free** in UK (gambling winnings not taxable)
- **Time:** 30–60 min/day after learning curve
- **Start:** Sign up → Complete training → Do welcome offers → Daily reloads
- **Risk:** Zero if followed correctly; mistakes = losses
- **Best for:** Anyone comfortable with basic maths and following instructions

#### 2. Mercor

- **What:** AI-powered talent marketplace — AI model training, code evaluation, RLHF
- **Earnings:** $40–150/hr (£32–120/hr equivalent); £500–2,000+/month
- **Payout:** Weekly via PayPal, no minimum
- **Onboarding:** 1–3 days — AI interview (Zara), fast matching
- **Tool:** Insightful time tracking (use dedicated device)
- **Best for:** Coding, finance, legal, STEM experts who want premium pay
- **Note:** Best pay-to-onboarding-speed ratio of any AI training platform

#### 3. DataAnnotation.tech

- **What:** AI model training — coding, writing, reasoning tasks
- **Earnings:** £20–40/hr (higher for specialised domains: finance, legal, medical, coding)
- **Payout:** Weekly (Tuesdays) via PayPal, no minimum
- **Barrier:** Qualification test (coding/English assessment)
- **Tip:** Domain expertise (finance, law, medicine, STEM) unlocks £30–50/hr tiers
- **Status:** Often has waitlist — sign up early

#### 3. UserTesting

- **What:** Record screen + voice while completing tasks on websites/apps
- **Earnings:** £10 per 20-min test (£30/hr equivalent), occasional £30–60 live interviews
- **Payout:** 7 days after test completion, PayPal, £10 minimum
- **Requirement:** Practice test + decent microphone + quiet environment
- **Tip:** High ratings → more invites. Think aloud constantly.

#### 4. User Interviews / Respondent.io

- **What:** 30–60 min video interviews for product research
- **Earnings:** £50–150 per study (£100–300/hr equivalent)
- **Payout:** 1–2 weeks via PayPal
- **Barrier:** Screening surveys; acceptance rate ~5–10%
- **Tip:** Complete profile fully; apply to studies matching your background

### TIER 2: MEDIUM £/HR (£8–15)

#### 5. Prolific (Academic Surveys)

- **What:** University research studies; higher ethics standards = better pay
- **Earnings:** £8–15/hr (typical £1–10 per study)
- **Payout:** Instant–1 day to PayPal, £6 minimum

- **Tip:** Maintain high approval rate (>95%) for more invites

#### 6. Alignerr (by Labelbox)

- **Earnings:** $20–50/hr (£16–40/hr); £200–800/month potential
- **Payout:** Bi-weekly via PayPal, $20 minimum

- **Note:** Newer platform = still scaling = less competition = opportunity

- **What:** AI operations — data review, content operations, team-based AI projects
- **Earnings:** $25–100/hr (£20–80/hr); £300–1,200/month potential

- **Onboarding:** Multi-stage interview, structured onboarding
- **Best for:** Those wanting long-term structured projects over one-off tasks

#### 8. TestingTime

#### 10. Respondent.io

## Tax & Legal (UK, 2026)

- **Trading Allowance:** First £1,000/year tax-free (covers most casual earnings)
- **Matched betting:** Gambling winnings = **tax-free** (HMRC confirmed)
- **Bank switching:** Treated as "other income" — covered by trading allowance
- **Above £1,000:** Register for Self Assessment; declare as miscellaneous income
- **Record-keeping:** Track date, platform, gross amount, fees, net received
- **Thresholds:** £12,570 personal allowance; £1,000 trading allowance; £1,000 property allowance

## Scam Avoidance Checklist

✅ **Legit platforms NEVER:**

- Ask for upfront payment to "unlock" jobs
- Require weird ID documents upfront
- Ask you to buy products to test them
- Promise guaranteed earnings
- Have no verifiable payment proofs online

✅ **Verify before joining:**

- Search "[Platform] review Reddit 2026"
- Check Trustpilot (3.5+ stars, 100+ reviews)
- Look for recent payment proofs on r/beermoneyuk
- Check if featured on MoneySavingExpert / Save the Student
