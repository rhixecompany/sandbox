## PART 1: Matched Betting

### What It Is
Matched betting uses free bet offers from bookmakers to guarantee profit. You place a "back" bet with the bookmaker and a "lay" bet on a betting exchange (Betfair/Smarkets) so you're covered regardless of the outcome. The free bet becomes pure profit.

**It is NOT gambling.** You win regardless of the result.

### What You Need

1. **A matched betting calculator** — free online versions exist, paid platforms have built-in ones
2. **Betting exchange account** — Smarket (easiest, lower commission) or Betfair
3. **Bookmaker accounts** — Ladbrokes, William Hill, Bet365, Paddy Power, etc.
4. **£50–100 float** — money to place qualifying bets (you get it back)

### Step-by-Step First Offer

Using a typical "Bet £10, Get £30 in Free Bets" offer:

1. Find a match on odds comparison (use the platform's tool)
2. Place £10 back bet at the bookmaker (odds ~3.0)
3. Place lay bet at the exchange to cover the bookmaker bet
4. Your qualifying loss is ~£0.20 (the cost of unlocking the offer)
5. Now you have £30 in free bets
6. Use the free bet on high odds (~10.0) and lay at the exchange
7. Profit: ~£25–28 from the free bet
8. Repeat for 20–30 welcome offers = £400–800 in the first week

### Tips for Success

- **Start with sign-up offers** — they're the biggest payouts
- **Follow each step exactly** — one mistake can cost you
- **Use a spreadsheet** to track offers, stakes, profits
- **Opt out of email promotions** to avoid temptation
- **Set aside 25% for tax** — wait, it's tax-free! (Gambling winnings aren't taxed in the UK)
- **Never gamble** — this is a mathematical exercise, not betting
- **Withdraw immediately** — don't leave money in bookmaker accounts

### Recommended First Steps

1. Sign up for a free trial of Outplayed or OddsMonkey
2. Open a Smarkets exchange account (fastest, lowest commission)
3. Fund with £100 (your float — you get it back)
4. Complete 3–5 sign-up offers in your first session
5. Profit: £100–200 in 2–3 hours

## PART 2: Bank Switching

### What It Is
UK banks pay you cash to switch your current account to them using the Current Account Switch Service (CASS). It's guaranteed by law — your direct debits, payments, and balance transfer automatically within 7 days.

### Current Offers (July 2026)

| Bank | Offer | Requirements |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Co-operative Bank | Up to **£300** | Eligible existing customers |
| Various | £175–220 | Switch + deposit + direct debits |

> **Check current offers:** money-saving blogs update these monthly. Search "best bank switching offers July 2026."

### The Strategy

1. **Get a "burner" account** — open a basic current account with a bank you don't care about (Monzo, Starling, or any high-street bank)
2. **Set up 2–3 direct debits** on it (e.g., pay 1p to a charity monthly, set up a PayPal subscription, small regular payment). You can also use services like Plum or Moneybox that create small direct debits automatically.
3. **Use CASS to switch** to whichever bank is offering the best cash incentive
4. **Collect the bonus** — typically within 7–30 days
5. **Repeat** — after receiving the bonus, switch the same burner account to the next bank offering the best deal

### How Many Times Can You Switch?

- Contracts usually say you can't switch back to the same bank within 12 months
- There are ~15–20 banks offering switching incentives each year
- Realistic: 3–4 switches per year = **£525–1,200/year in free money**

### Tips

- Keep the burner account open permanently — never close it
- Track switch dates and bonus receipt dates in a spreadsheet
- Read the T&Cs carefully — some require minimum deposits or specific number of DDs
- Use the 1p charity DD trick (set up a monthly £0.01 DD to a charity)
- Don't close your main bank account — use the burner for switching only
