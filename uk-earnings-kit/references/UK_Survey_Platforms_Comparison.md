## How to read this table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | 🥇 Best = £6+/hr real-world; 🥈 Good = £3-6/hr; 🥉 OK = £2-3/hr; 💎 Specialist = high ceiling but intermittent |
| **£/hr** | Realistic steady-state (not signup-bonus inflated), from user reports & reviews |
| **Disqual rate** | How often you start a survey and get kicked out before finishing |
| **Min payout** | Lowest cash/PayPal withdrawal |
| **Payout methods** | Cash options listed; most also do gift cards |
| **In existing kit?** | Whether it's in your current rotation already |

## RECOMMENDED STARTER PACK (Add these 6 first)

For best coverage and earnings beyond Attapoll:

1. **OnePoll** — Best reliable £/hr (£4-5) for quick UK surveys. £5 bonus.
2. **Prime Opinion** — Low threshold (£3.95), fast payouts, decent volume.
3. **iSay (IPSOS)** — Trusted name, £4-5/hr, quality surveys.
4. **User Interviews** — Sign up for occasional high-pay studies (£50-150/hr).
5. **PaidViewpoint** — Zero disqualification frustration; always earn something.
6. **Superpay.me** — Ultra-low $1 cashout for quick small earnings.
