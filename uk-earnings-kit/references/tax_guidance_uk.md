# UK Tax Guidance for Side Earnings (2026/27 Tax Year)

> **Tax Year:** 6 April 2026 – 5 April 2027  
> **Register by:** 5 October 2027  
> **File & Pay by:** 31 January 2028  
> **Keep records until:** 31 January 2033 (6 years)

---

## The Three Allowances That Protect You

| Allowance                      | Amount                         | What It Covers                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trading Allowance**          | £1,000/year                    | **All** self-employment/misc trading income combined |
| **Property Allowance**         | £1,000/year                    | Rental income (not relevant here)                    |
| **Personal Savings Allowance** | £1,000 (basic) / £500 (higher) | Bank interest, bank switch bonuses                   |

**Key rule:** You can use **both** Trading Allowance AND Personal Savings Allowance in the same year — they're separate.

## How the Trading Allowance Works

### Scenario A: Total trading income ≤ £1,000

- **No registration needed**
- **No tax return needed**
- **No tax payable**
- Keep records anyway (6 years)

### Scenario B: Total trading income > £1,000

You have **two choices**:

#### Option 1: Claim Trading Allowance (simpler)

- Deduct £1,000 from gross income
- Pay tax on the remainder
- **Cannot claim actual expenses** if you use this
- Best if expenses < £1,000

#### Option 2: Claim Actual Expenses (better if high costs)

- Deduct actual allowable expenses
- Pay tax on (gross – expenses)
- **Cannot claim Trading Allowance** if you do this
- Best if expenses > £1,000 (e.g., matched betting subscription, equipment, travel)

**Example:**

- Gross survey/testing/AI income: £2,500
- Matched betting sub: £360/year
- Equipment (mic, webcam): £150
- **Option 1:** Taxable = £2,500 – £1,000 = £1,500
- **Option 2:** Taxable = £2,500 – £360 – £150 = £1,990 (worse in this case)
- **Choose Option 1** (allowance) unless expenses exceed £1,000

## Matched Betting: Why It's Tax-Free

> **HMRC Manual BIM22015:** "Betting and gambling winnings are not taxable. This applies to both professional and amateur gamblers."

- No need to declare matched betting profits
- No Trading Allowance used
- **Keep records anyway** — if HMRC asks, you prove it's matched betting, not trading
- Subscription fees (Outplayed £360/yr) are **not deductible** against other income

## Bank Switching: Treated as Savings Interest

- Cash incentive = "payment for switching" = savings interest
- Covered by **Personal Savings Allowance (PSA)**
  - Basic rate (£12,571–£50,270): £1,000
  - Higher rate (£50,271–£125,140): £500
  - Additional rate (>£125,140): £0
- Most people won't exceed PSA with just bank switches
- If you have other savings interest, it all counts toward PSA

---

## Crypto Rewards (Freecash, etc.)

- Receiving crypto = **no tax event**
- **Selling/swapping/spending** crypto = Capital Gains Tax event
- CGT Annual Exempt Amount: **£3,000** (2026/27)
- Track: acquisition date, GBP value at receipt, disposal date, GBP value at disposal
- Pooling rules apply (Section 104 pooling)

## When to Register for Self Assessment

**Must register if ANY:**

- Trading income > £1,000 (before expenses/allowance)
- You want to claim expenses instead of allowance
- You have other reasons (rental, foreign income, etc.)
- HMRC asks you to

**Deadline:** 5 October following the tax year (5 Oct 2027 for 2026/27)

**How:** GOV.UK → "Register for Self Assessment" → Get UTR → Activate online account

- 6% on £12,570 – £50,270

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Thinking matched betting is taxable** — it's not
2. **Not tracking bank switch bonuses** — they're interest, not trading
3. **Claiming both Trading Allowance AND expenses** — pick one
4. **Forgetting crypto disposals** — every swap/sale is a CGT event
5. **Missing the registration deadline** — £100 penalty + daily penalties
6. **Not keeping records 6 years** — HMRC can enquire back that far
7. **Mixing personal/business accounts** — makes tracking impossible
