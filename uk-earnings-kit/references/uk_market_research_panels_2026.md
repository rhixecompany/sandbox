## Detailed Platform Profiles

### 1. User Interviews ⭐⭐

**URL:** https://www.userinterviews.com  
**Type:** Focus groups, 1:1 interviews, usability tests, surveys  
**Pay range:** Typically **£40–£120/hr** ($50–$150/hr); focus groups $50–$150/hr range most common  
**Frequency:** High — thousands of new studies added monthly. New studies posted daily  
**Selection rate:** ~5–10% (competitive, but worth it given the rates)  
**Min payout:** None per study; you get paid what the study offers  
**Payout methods:** PayPal cash or gift cards (1,000+ options via Tremendous). Usually paid within 24 hrs of completion  
**UK availability:** ✅ Yes — UK residents welcome, GBP studies available. Studies in £, $, CAD, EUR  
**Bonus:** £8 signup bonus with referral link  
**Tldr:** Widely considered the best platform for paid research. Used by 6M+ participants. Reliable, good communication, quick payment.

### 2. Respondent.io ⭐⭐

**URL:** https://www.respondent.io  
**Type:** Focus groups, 1:1 interviews, diary studies, usability tests, surveys  
**Pay range:** Typically **£60–£300/hr** ($75–$400+ per session). Averages ~$95/project. Focus groups $75–$200/session standard; B2B/professional $150–$750/hr  
**Frequency:** High — 4,000+ research projects posted monthly. 4.3M+ verified participants globally  
**Selection rate:** ~8% (1,200 screeners for 10 studies is typical)  
**Min payout:** None — you're paid the study fee  
**Payout methods:** PayPal (via Tremendous). Paid within 8–10 days. Takes 5% processing fee  
**UK availability:** ✅ Yes — UK participants welcome. In-person sessions in London and other major cities  
**Tldr:** Great for B2B professionals. Higher pay for industry specialists. $35M+ paid out to participants. Reliable but competitive.

### 3. Prolific

**URL:** https://www.prolific.com  
**Type:** Academic & AI research studies (surveys, experiments, AI training tasks)  
**Pay range:** **£6–£12/hr** typical. Absolute min £6/hr; recommended £9/hr. Most studies $10–15/hr  
**Frequency:** Very high — studies available daily if you have a good profile. Can earn £25–£150/month depending on demographics  
**Selection rate:** High — less competitive than focus group platforms but depends on demographics matching  
**Min payout:** £5  
**Payout methods:** PayPal. Usually instant or within hours  
**UK availability:** ✅ Yes — founded at Oxford University. UK participants get first pick of many studies  
**Tldr:** Best for steady, reliable survey income. Not as high-paying as focus groups but very consistent. £6/hr absolute minimum guarantee makes it ethical. 10M+ participants.

### 4. Take Part In Research

**URL:** https://takepartinresearch.co.uk  
**Type:** Focus groups, telephone interviews, online interviews, face-to-face, diary studies  
**Pay range:** Online groups £40–£60; in-person £60–£150 per session (~£30–£60/hr)  
**Frequency:** Moderate — new projects listed every week. You can browse and apply  
**Selection rate:** Moderate — UK-focused so less competition  
**Min payout:** None  
**Payout methods:** Bank transfer, vouchers  
**UK availability:** ✅ Yes — UK-only platform. Covers all regions  
**Tldr:** Excellent UK-specific platform. Transparent with live listing of current projects with exact pay. Very user-friendly.

### 5. People for Research

## Tips for Maximising Earnings

1. **Join multiple panels** — spread the net wide. Sign up for 10+ platforms
2. **Complete your profile fully** — incomplete profiles get fewer invites
3. **Respond fast** — focus group slots fill within hours of being sent out
4. **Be honest in screeners** — don't lie; you'll get screened out later and waste everyone's time
5. **Prioritise focus groups over surveys** — focus groups pay £40–£150/hr vs surveys at £6–£12/hr
6. **Check emails daily** — most invites go out by email and slots fill quickly
7. **Set up a dedicated email** — for organising research panel invites
8. **Follow social media** — Research Opinions, Angelfish, and others post opportunities on Instagram/Facebook before emailing

## Disqualification Rates

Most focus group platforms have **80–95% disqualification rates** after screening — this is normal. You're being screened for very specific demographics. Don't get discouraged.

**By platform type:**

- **User Interviews / Respondent:** ~90–95% screened out
- **Angelfish / Give Opinions / Take Part In Research:** ~70–80% (less competition as UK-only)
- **Prolific:** ~30–50% (much higher acceptance, lower pay)
- **Wynter:** ~60–70% (B2B niche)
