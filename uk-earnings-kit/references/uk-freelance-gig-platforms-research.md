### Practical Considerations

1. **IR35** — Many construction freelancers operate outside IR35 via limited companies. Ensure your contract review accounts for this.
2. **CIS (Construction Industry Scheme)** — Construction work in the UK is subject to CIS tax deductions (20% or 30%). Platforms like Checkatrade, MyBuilder, and Sitemanagers.co.uk are CIS-aware.
3. **Insurance** — Most trade platforms (Checkatrade, MyBuilder, Bark) require public liability insurance (£1M–£5M typical).
4. **Qualifications** — SMSTS (Site Management Safety Training Scheme) or SSSTS (Supervisor) are expected for site roles.
5. **Platform stacking** — Most successful UK tradespeople use 2–3 platforms simultaneously (e.g., Checkatrade + MyBuilder + Bark) to maintain steady lead flow.
