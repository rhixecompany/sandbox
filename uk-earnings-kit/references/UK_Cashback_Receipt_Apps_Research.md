## Quick Summary for Alexa

You already use TopCashback, Quidco, Complete Savings, and Jam Doughnut. Below are **15+ additional platforms** worth layering on top. Most combine well with what you already have. The biggest wins for UK users in 2026:

- **Gift card stacking:** EverUp and Cheddar work alongside JamDoughnut — compare rates before buying.

## Stacking Strategy (How to Layer Multiple Apps on One Shop)

This is where the real value is. From a single shop, you can claim cashback from **multiple sources**:

```
Example: £50 Tesco Weekly Shop

1. EverUp/JamDoughnut: Buy £50 Tesco gift card @ 3% cashback   → +£1.50
2. Pay with cashback debit card (e.g. Chase 1%)                → +£0.50
3. Clear!: Scan receipt for 10p                                 → +£0.10
4. Shopmium/GreenJinn: If any items match offers (£1-3 each)    → +£2.00 avg
5. Airtime Rewards: Already linked, may trigger                 → +£0.50
6. Nectar/Clubcard: As normal (free points)                     → ~£0.50

Total: ~£5.10 back on a £50 shop = ~10% effective discount
```

## Key Takeaways for Alexa

1. **Best new additions to try:** EverUp (often beats JamDoughnut on rates), Clear! (10p/receipt, instant), Shopmium (£1 referral + weekly freebies)
2. **Set-and-forget:** Airtime Rewards — link card once, cashback auto-tracks to phone bill
3. **Highest passive earner:** Cheddar gift card cashback (up to 10%) combined with Airtime Rewards
4. **Receipt stacking works:** The same receipt can go into Clear! + Shoppix + any product apps (Shopmium etc.) — triple-dip on one shop
5. **Avoid:** Fetch Rewards (US-only), ZipZero (dead), Amazon Shopper Panel (closed UK)
6. **Watch out for:** CheckoutSmart's 5% withdrawal fee (wait until £20+), NX Rewards £18/mo sub trap (set a calendar reminder if you try it)
