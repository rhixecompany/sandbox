## Section 3: Summary Recommendations

### Best for Regular Income (Batch Tasks)

1. **Roamler** — Most consistent task availability, good support, free pint jobs
2. **Shepper** — Regular National Lottery and retail checks, reliable pay
3. **BeMyEye** — High volume of quick shelf-photo tasks (good filler)
4. **Market Force** — Nationwide coverage, well-established

### Best Pay Per Task

1. **Performance in People** — Up to £80 for video mystery shops
2. **Tern** — Up to £40+ for complex tasks; weekly pay
3. **Serve Legal** — Up to £40/store for age checks
4. **GBW** — ~£35 for McDonald's evaluations

### Best for Dining Out (Free Meals)

1. **HGEM Mystery Dining** — Free meal for two + £10-20
2. **iShopFor Ipsos** — Greene King pub visits

### Best Physical / Gig Tasks

1. **TaskRabbit** — Highest earning potential if skilled (assembly, handyman)
2. **Deliveroo/Uber Eats** — Steady demand, weekly pay
3. **Airtasker** — Flexible, wide variety of local tasks

### Key Tips

- **Sign up for multiple apps** and check daily — best tasks go fast
- **Batch tasks** in same geographic area to maximise hourly rate
- Experienced users report **£25+/hour** average on well-chosen tasks
- Many tasks **reimburse purchases** (products, meals) on top of the fee
- Keep a spreadsheet for **tax purposes** (£1,000+ needs HMRC declaration)
- Mileage (45p/mile) can be claimed as a legitimate business expense
