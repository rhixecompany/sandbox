## 2. Requirements Summary — What You Need for Each Switch

Every CASS-based switch offer requires:
- **Two active Direct Debits** (most common requirement)
- **A qualifying minimum deposit** (usually £1,250–£2,000)
- **App usage / online banking registration**
- **No recent account** with that bank group

> ⚠️ **Important:** HSBC and First Direct are the same banking group — you cannot claim both bonuses. Same goes for NatWest/RBS/Ulster Bank (all RBS Group).

## 8. Important Caveats

- All offers subject to status, eligibility, and T&Cs
- Most offers exclude those who have **previously received a switch bonus** from that bank
- **Hard credit searches** are run when applying — can affect credit score temporarily
- CASS (Current Account Switch Service) guarantees transfer within 7 working days
- Always verify directly on the bank's website before applying — offers can be withdrawn at any time
- This research is current as of 28 July 2026 but offers change frequently
