## Tax & Compliance (UK)

- **Trading allowance**: £1,000/year tax-free for casual income
- **Self-assessment**: Required if >£1,000 or sole trade
- **Platform reporting**: Some platforms (Upwork, Fiverr) report to HMRC
- **VAT**: Only if >£90,000 turnover
- **Reference**: [HMRC Self-employment](https://www.gov.uk/working-for-yourself)

See `references/tax_guidance.md` for full breakdown.
