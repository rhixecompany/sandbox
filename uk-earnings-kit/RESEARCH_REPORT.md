## Scoring Methodology

### Weighted Criteria
| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Effective £/hour | 30% | Real earnings after time spent |
| Reliability | 20% | Consistency of work and payments |
| UK Accessibility | 15% | Can UK users access easily |
| Entry Barrier | 10% | How hard to start earning |
| Scalability | 10% | Can earnings grow over time |
| Tax Efficiency | 10% | Tax treatment (trading allowance, VAT) |
| Time to First £ | 5% | How fast can you cash out |

### Auto-Elimination Criteria
- Pay < £5/hr effective
- No UK payout method (PayPal UK, UK bank transfer, Wise GBP)
- >30% disqualification rate on surveys
- Trustpilot < 3.5 or < 100 reviews
- Invitation-only with no public waitlist
- Known payment delays > 14 days
- Requires US SSN/ITIN/W-8BEN without UK alternative
