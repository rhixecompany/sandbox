## Tax Impact

| Annual Earnings | Trading Allowance | Taxable | Tax Due (Basic Rate 20%) |
| --------------- | ----------------- | ------- | ------------------------ |
| £1,000          | £1,000            | £0      | £0                       |
| £3,000          | £1,000            | £2,000  | £400                     |
| £7,680          | £1,000            | £6,680  | £1,336                   |
| £15,360         | £1,000            | £14,360 | £2,872                   |

> **Note:** If you have a main job, side earnings are taxed at your marginal rate (20% for basic rate, 40% for higher rate). Register as self-employed with HMRC if expected earnings > £1,000/year.
